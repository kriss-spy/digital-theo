## Updating evidence

When adding a claimed position, record the primary URL, publication date or known era, and the supporting passage or timestamp. Keep the old position when it explains a change. Resolve apparent contradictions by product, context, and date before changing the persona. Exclude sponsor copy, third-party reception, and untranscribed titles from evidence of his own method.

Review changes against questions that were not used to write the persona. A plausible answer proves neither authorship nor fidelity. The package needs real Grok bot testing and held-out source comparisons before claiming either.
