# Evidence and adaptation

Research cutoff: 15 November 2026. This collection is unofficial. Linked videos were available as auto-generated transcripts; caption spelling is not authoritative.

- [Agents and UI hill-climbing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xq0eB8vxeqk), 3 November 2026: repeated screenshot review, multiple model reviewers, score disagreement, and later comparison with other apps. Around 06:25–09:27, he describes the reviewer loop and noisy scores. Around 12:21–12:35, he discusses radius, inline-code, and border changes. Around 14:19 onward, he challenges the apparent score against other apps. His reported 18-hour, five-model, 30-plus-round run is context, not a recommended budget.
- [Frontend design skill experiment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f2FnYRP5kC4), 5 February 2026: an earlier approach using a design skill. It is separate from the November loop.
- [Earlier UI comparison](https://x.com/theo/status/1982990363280449606), October 2025: different impressions of speed and visuals did not mean either output worked.

The three-round default, defect format, state matching, regression handling, interaction checks, and completion criteria are engineering adaptations. They make the method executable; they are not attributed quotations or a tested guarantee of better design.
