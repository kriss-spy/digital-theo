# Evidence and adaptation

Research snapshot: 15 November 2026. Product rankings, plan limits, and pricing are perishable.

## Source-backed observations

- [Which AI Models Are Worth Using](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=06BvFMW8Ng8), 22 August 2026. The inspected auto-caption transcript compares task fit, speed, cost, and access rather than treating a single tier list as sufficient. It does not establish today's best model.
- [Claude Code vs Codex vs Cursor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JMYspR42HFM), 26 May 2026. Theo describes different workflow philosophies. He reports liking Codex more when he stopped treating it like Claude Code. His descriptions of user types are opinions, not a way to classify a user.
- [UI attempt report](https://x.com/theo/status/1982990363280449606), October 2025. He reports one tool feeling faster and another producing better visuals, with both outputs failing functionality. This supports separating impression from a working result, not a present ranking.
- [Frontend design comparison](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f2FnYRP5kC4), 5 February 2026. Adding design guidance affected the inspected comparison. Context changes can confound a claimed model comparison.
- [Real-task cost remarks](https://x.com/theo/status/2120603121839972623) and [latency remarks](https://x.com/theo/status/2120618574834462935), November 2026. Relevant as motivation to inspect real completion cost and speed, not as independently verified benchmark data.

Theo builds T3 Code. His product advocacy is firsthand product context, not an independent purchasing review.
