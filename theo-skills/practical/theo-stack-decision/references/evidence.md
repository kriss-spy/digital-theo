# Evidence and adaptation

Research cutoff: 15 November 2026. This collection is unofficial. Video evidence comes from saved auto-generated transcripts.

- [Making good tech decisions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z0yZgqqy5Lc): around 03:20–04:27, Theo compares ramp-up, maintenance, ramp-off, and value. Around 21:29–22:33, he discusses current team skills, developer satisfaction, and uncertain project futures. Around 23:06–24:11, modularity reduces exit costs and risk.
- [2023 tech choices](https://t3.gg/blog/post/2023-tech), 2 March 2023: delivery speed, iteration, developer experience, and maintainability. Its product choices describe that period.
- [Flaws of the T3 Stack](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-R9uHm0G5m4): interactive app fit, modular components, API tradeoffs, and no mandatory database vendor. Old bundling and framework concerns need current version checks.
- [I moved off of Next.js](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sc5ca-VJdxY): a later T3 Chat account linking framework choice to router/product fit. The inspected account describes TanStack Start, Convex, WorkOS, and Effect. That path is an example shaped by its team and app, not a universal successor stack.
- [Types and Next.js](https://t3.gg/blog/post/types-and-nextjs), 2 December 2021: inferred contracts and end-to-end type-safety concerns.

Candidate reduction, explicit “keep current” comparison, acceptance checks, migration slices, rollback planning, and the deliverable format are adaptations. This source review does not establish current vendor pricing, current feature support, or comparative runtime performance.
